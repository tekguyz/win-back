Drafted 2026-11-18 from `tekguyz-one/projects/win-back/about.md`, which wins if the two disagree.

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to a TEKGUYZ demo: owners of AC and heating companies with yearly service, and of dental and vet offices with recalls. The owner reads a short report and approves messages one at a time; the customer gets a short message signed by the owner.

## Product Purpose

A landing page and a one-click demo of win-back messages. Line: "Bring back the customers who stopped calling." Success: an owner sees that the customers who stopped coming back are found in their own records, explained in plain words, and written to only with their approval.

## Positioning

It starts from the business's own past customers only, never strangers, and nothing goes out until the owner approves it, one customer at a time.

## Operating Context

The demo runs as Buttonwood Air, a made-up AC company in South Florida, with its past-customer records. The visitor scans the records, reads the report, approves messages and watches a reply book a visit. Everything stays in the visitor's browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Groups lapsed customers: missed this year's tune-up, maintenance plan ran out, one visit and never back.
- One draft per customer; approve, edit or skip each. There is no "approve all".
- Never messages anyone who asked not to get texts; never sends anything in the demo ("Sent" is a status in the browser).

## Brand Commitments

- The shipped look in `DESIGN.md` is binding. This is a shipped demo: never restyle it or roll a new visual world unless the founder names this demo and asks for that (`claude-config/LOOKS.md`, rule 1).
- Say: "Bring back the customers who stopped calling." "Nothing goes out until the owner approves it, one customer at a time." Never say anything is sent, that Buttonwood Air is real, or that there is an approve-all button.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. Never show a real business, a TEKGUYZ price, a statistic or a phone number.

## Product Principles

- The owner stays in control of every message.
- The report explains who and why in plain words before asking for anything.
- Honest about the demo: every simulated step says so.
